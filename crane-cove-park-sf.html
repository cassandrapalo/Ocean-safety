Sheltered bay shore · wind from NOAA Buoy 46026

Why no swell numbers. This shore is sheltered inside the bay, so offshore swell readings do not describe the water here. The card shows wind and tide instead.

Wind. The direction the wind is coming from, measured at NOAA Buoy 46026 offshore, with speed averaged over an eight minute period and the highest gust in that time, in miles per hour. Wind on the bay can differ from the buoy. This shore faces E. Onshore means the wind is blowing from the water toward this shore, offshore means it is blowing from the land out over the water.

Tides. NOAA's predicted high and low tides for station 9414290, San Francisco (Golden Gate), in feet above mean lower low water. These are predictions, not measurements.

Sources: NOAA National Data Buoy Center; NOAA CO-OPS tide predictions.

Known Hazards at Crane Cove
Seawall & Riprap

Swimming prohibited by the Port: jagged armor stone and submerged industrial remnants.

Source: sfport.com · sfpuc.gov

Boat Traffic & Wakes

Adjacent active shipyard and tugboat turbulence. Planned ferry terminal (2027).

Source: sfport.com · sfpuc.gov

Water Quality

Combined sewer outfall near Mariposa St discharges during storms.

Source: sfport.com · sfpuc.gov

No Lifeguards

No lifeguards; swimming prohibited (wading only to nearest buoy line).

Managed by Port of San Francisco

Ocean Emergency
911 USCG San Francisco (415) 399-3547
01Check the tide.

The tide chart in the card above shows today's highs and lows. Bay water moves with the tide, and an outgoing tide pulls water toward the open ocean.

02Bay water is cold year round.

Cold water shock can take your breath away within the first minute. Enter slowly, and do not swim alone.

03Watch for boats and wakes.

Boat wakes can reach the shore long after a vessel has passed.

04Keep dogs on leash.

Keep dogs on leash and away from the water's edge.

05Tell someone your plans.

Leave your destination and expected return time with someone before you go.

About this data

Wind: NOAA Buoy 46026 — San Francisco Bar. Tides: NOAA station 9414290, San Francisco (Golden Gate). NWS zone CAZ006.

This tool displays publicly available NOAA buoy and tide data. It is provided for informational purposes only and does not constitute safety advice. Data may be delayed, incomplete, or inaccurate. Conditions at the water can differ from buoy readings. This tool does not account for all hazards. Use your own judgment. When in doubt, stay out.
